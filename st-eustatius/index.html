The Neighbouring Island

St. Eustatius

A quiet, unspoiled gem just south of Saba — known to locals as Statia, the historic "Golden Rock" of the Caribbean Netherlands.

About the Island

A Hidden Gem of the Caribbean

St. Eustatius — "Statia" to those who know it — is a special municipality of the Netherlands, sharing that status with Saba and Bonaire. At roughly 21 km² and home to around 3,100 residents, it is small, unhurried, and refreshingly uncommercial.

The island is crowned by The Quill, a near-perfect green peak whose crater holds a lush tropical rainforest. To the north, the rugged Boven hills meet the sea. Between them sits a gentle central plain where most of island life unfolds.

Like Saba, Statia has no high-rises and no crowds — just dramatic scenery, a deep sense of history, and a close-knit community.

What Makes Statia

A Different Kind of Caribbean

The Quill

A near-perfect green peak rising some 600 metres above the sea, with a forested crater you can hike down into — one of the Caribbean's most distinctive walks.

World-Class Diving

A protected marine park with healthy reefs, dramatic underwater pinnacles and 18th-century shipwrecks — quiet sites that divers often have to themselves.

The Golden Rock

Once one of the busiest trading ports on earth, Statia wears its history openly — colonial ruins, cobbled streets, and centuries-old fortifications.

A Storied Capital

Oranjestad & the First Salute

Statia's capital, Oranjestad, splits into a clifftop Upper Town and a seaside Lower Town, linked by a historic cobbled path. Above it all stands Fort Oranje, looking out over the anchorage where hundreds of ships once moored.

It was from that fort, on 16 November 1776, that St. Eustatius became the first foreign territory to formally salute the flag of the new United States — the event still remembered today as the "First Salute."

Wander Oranjestad now and you'll find museums, restored warehouses, and quiet streets where that long history is never far from view.

Ask About Statia Property

St. Eustatius — Common Questions

Can you buy property on St. Eustatius?
Yes. Like Saba, St. Eustatius is a special municipality of the Netherlands with no nationality restrictions on property ownership. Saban Realty serves Statia alongside Saba — contact us about current opportunities.
How do you get to St. Eustatius?
Statia is reached by a short Winair flight from Sint Maarten (SXM) into F.D. Roosevelt Airport (EUX). The US dollar is the local currency, the same as on Saba.
What is The Quill?
The Quill is the island's signature green peak — a near-perfect cone rising some 600 metres above the sea, its crater holding a lush tropical rainforest. It anchors a national park that draws hikers and nature lovers.
What is the diving like on Statia?
St. Eustatius is known for protected, uncrowded dive sites — dramatic underwater pinnacles, healthy reefs, and historic shipwrecks within a marine park, making it a favourite for divers seeking quiet waters.
