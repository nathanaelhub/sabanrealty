Four villages · Five square miles

Pick a hill, then a house.

Saba is just five square miles, divided into four small villages — each with its own character. The Bottom is the island capital; Windwardside holds the galleries, shops and restaurants; St. John’s is home to the island’s primary and secondary schools; and Zion’s Hill (Hell’s Gate) is the first village you reach from the airport. Find the one that feels like home, then find the house.
