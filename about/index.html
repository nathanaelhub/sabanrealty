Discover Saba

The Unspoiled Queen

Saba is a 5 km² island in the Caribbean Netherlands — a special municipality of the Netherlands. Rising dramatically from the sea, it's crowned by Mount Scenery, the highest point in the Kingdom of the Netherlands.

With no beaches but spectacular diving, world-class hiking, and authentic Caribbean charm, Saba attracts those seeking something different. The island's four villages — The Bottom (the capital), Windwardside, St. Johns, and Zion's Hill — are connected by "The Road," an engineering marvel carved into the mountainside.

Property here is limited and precious, making it an ideal destination for those seeking privacy, natural beauty, and a genuine community connection.

About Saba & Saban Realty — Common Questions

Where is Saba?
Saba is a five-square-mile island in the Caribbean Netherlands, a special municipality of the Netherlands, lying about 28 miles south of Sint Maarten. It's one of the most unspoiled islands in the Caribbean.
How do you get to Saba?
Most visitors fly in from Sint Maarten (SXM) on a short Winair flight of about 15 minutes, landing at Juancho E. Yrausquin Airport. A ferry service also connects the two islands.
What makes Saban Realty different?
We combine deep local roots on Saba with the reach of the global RE/MAX network — pairing on-the-ground island knowledge with worldwide marketing to connect the right buyers and sellers.
