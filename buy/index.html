Buying Property on Saba — Common Questions

Can foreigners buy property on Saba?
Yes. Saba is a special municipality of the Netherlands, and there are no nationality restrictions on property ownership in the Caribbean Netherlands. You do not need to be a Dutch or EU citizen to buy a home or land here.
Do I need to live on Saba or have residency to buy?
No. You can own property on Saba without living here, and buying property does not by itself grant residency. If you're planning to relocate, contact our team and we'll walk you through the current residency steps.
What currency are prices listed in?
The US dollar is the official currency of the Caribbean Netherlands, so all of our listings are priced in USD.
How do I get to Saba to view a property?
Saba is reached from Sint Maarten (SXM) — a short Winair flight of about 15 minutes into Juancho E. Yrausquin Airport, or by ferry. We're happy to help coordinate viewings around your travel dates.
What does the buying process involve?
In broad strokes: we help you submit an offer, a civil-law notary handles the legal transfer of title, and closing costs (such as transfer tax and notary fees) apply on top of the purchase price. Reach out for a clear cost estimate on any specific property.
